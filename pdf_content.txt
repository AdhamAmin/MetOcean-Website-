COMPANY OVERVIEW
About	MetOcean
MetOcean Environmental Services & Solutions is an accredited house of
expertise recognized by the Egyptian Ministry of Environment. Founded as a
dynamic environmental consultancy, the company has grown steadily around a
multidisciplinary team with more than fifteen years of combined experience in
environmental management, quality and compliance.
MetOcean is deeply versed in national, regional and international environmental
legislation, and works closely with clients and stakeholders to deliver sound
environmental management — from small-scale activities to major infrastructure
across marine, coastal, industrial and service developments.
15+ Years of Experience
600+ Projects Delivered
6 Countries Served
